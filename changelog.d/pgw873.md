- **pgw#873 + pgw#893 §2 + pgw#881 A: the reconnect path loses its last magic number, and the worker credential loses its second home.** The auth-failure ladder no longer kills a worker on `3 consecutive UNAUTHENTICATED over 60 s`. Both premises under that pair were measured false: the "pg blip" it waited out is unreachable (the hub returns `Unavailable` for transient store errors and reserves `Unauthenticated` for genuine verdicts, #539), and on a **pod** the worker is not the actuator at all — `worker_wedge.go` revokes the token and closes the pod-hour ledger from the side that can see the pod, so a worker-side exit could only fire earlier than the authority and take a running mint with it. Now: a pod NEVER self-terminates on auth and emits a typed `auth_verdict_deferred_to_hub` event instead of going quiet; a **pod-less** worker (which has no hub-side actuator — `noteWedgeStreak` returns immediately on an empty `RunpodPodID`) escalates on EVIDENCE, the identical verdict on the identical `jti` seen twice, with no threshold and no window. Separately, `Transport._worker_jwt` is deleted: the stream-local rotation cache was read BEFORE `worker_credential`, and the rotation handler assigned it before publishing process-wide inside a swallowing `except` — so a failed publish left one stream authenticating with a credential no other hub dial in the process could see, which is the pgw#848 split restored one level down. `worker_credential` is now the single home, written first and unswallowed.

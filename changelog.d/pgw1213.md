- **pgw#1213: the key scheme is `cg-key-v1`.** The per-entry key scheme minted by
  `cell_key` is renamed `ek1` -> `cg-key-v1`, landed before the `v0.114.0` tag
  existed and before anything published, so no artifact was ever addressed by
  `ek1` — the 0.114.0 wheel ships `cg-key-v1`. `is_key` generalizes with it: the
  scheme token now contains hyphens, so the grammar is `<scheme>-<56 lowercase
  hex>` matched from the RIGHT (the fixed-width digest is anchored to the end and
  everything before it is the scheme) and no reader may split a key on `-` or
  slice a fixed prefix width off one.
- **pgw#1213 / th#1897: the compiled-graph key grammar is a cross-repo contract, and
  the ref fragment is wide enough to carry a key.** `MAX_FRAGMENT_LEN` moves 64 -> 96
  in the new leaf module `gen_worker.refgrammar`, mirroring tensorhub's
  `internal/refgrammar.MaxFragmentLen`: a key IS the `#fragment` of
  `root/family-<f>#<key>`, and `cg-key-v1-` + 56 hex is 66 bytes, so under the old cap
  `parse_cell_ref` raised and returned `("", "")` — a pod could not name the family of
  the artifact it had just armed, with no hub involved. `is_key` is scheme-AGNOSTIC
  (th#1183): it refuses SHAPE, never scheme, so a newer fleet's key stays addressable
  by an older hub and the two can ship in different windows. Both grammars are pinned
  by `tests/testdata/compiled_graph_key_vectors.json` (26 vectors, vendored
  byte-identically in tensorhub, digest-fenced by `scripts/check_grammar_corpus_digest.py`
  in `fast gates` and by `scripts/grammar-vector-drift.sh` from the hub side). The arm
  token's disjointness from the key space now rides its DIGEST WIDTH (64 hex, not 56)
  rather than its `arm2-` prefix, which a scheme-agnostic grammar cannot honour.

- **pgw#1000: the export-parallel gate is deleted, and the measurement it was
  waiting for turns out to have been the wrong number all along.**
  `GEN_WORKER_AOT_EXPORT_PARALLEL` shipped OFF for three releases behind a
  stated reason — *"width is bounded by the EXPORT-phase footprint, which
  nobody has measured... until then this refuses to guess and stays OFF"*. The
  footprint was not unmeasured. `timings["export_peak_device_bytes"]` is
  `max_memory_allocated` over the ENTIRE serial export loop against a resident
  pipeline — **15.4 GiB** on the only complete 36-entry sdxl AOT mint that has
  ever finished — so `width_for` was being asked *"how many 15.4 GiB workers
  fit beside a resident mint child"* and correctly answered **1**, on every
  card, forever. A flag guarding a computation with one possible answer is not
  a safety mechanism; it is a feature that never ran.
- `aot_mint._ExportFootprint` measures the right quantity: **one row's delta
  over the resident baseline**, which is what a worker adds to a card that
  already holds the module it traces. Reported as `per_export_device_bytes`
  beside — never instead of — the phase high-water, because the two size
  different things (a pool vs. the mint child itself) and conflating them is
  the defect. The stale key is *disconnected*, not merely deprecated: a mint
  that ships only `export_peak_device_bytes` now yields width 1.
- **The width decision lives in code, with no env gate.** Width 1 is the
  natural floor when the budget says so, through named terms. The budget is
  pgw#992's, unchanged — `total − resident co-tenant − own device high-water` —
  so an export worker is priced exactly as an entry-compile child is priced,
  on the same card, beside the same residents. The census is taken *before* the
  export phase: `total − co-tenant − own` collapses to exactly `free` when the
  census and the high-water coincide, so a census read at decision time would
  price nothing at all.
- **`aot_export_reuse` (591 lines) is deleted, not re-gated.** It could never
  fire: `export_reuse_eligible` measured **0.0 on sdxl** (banked, two pods —
  its Transformer2DModel bakes the sequence length and the spatial extents) and
  **0.0 on micro-diffusion** (measured this session). Two structurally
  different families, both ineligible, and no family has ever been eligible.
  `GEN_WORKER_AOT_EXPORT_REUSE` goes with it, along with its allowlist entries,
  its named-threat justification and its owned-name census rows.
- Why this matters, from the same mint: `export_s 3891.82` of `total_s
  9546.65` — **65 minutes of a 2 h 43 m mint that pgw#809's K-wide compile pool
  divides by one.** It is the largest serial term left in the AOT mint.

- **pgw#883 / pgw#914 / pgw#941: three SDK surfaces with no live consumer are deleted.** (1) The swap-latency benchmark and its `gen_worker.diagnostics.SwapLatencyDiagnostics` endpoint wrapper are gone. The wrapper's delivery contract was subclass adoption and the fleet subclass census was **zero** for the whole 0.89 → 0.112 window, so no release could publish a `swap-latency` function and no such job row could exist; ordinary production traffic is the canonical speed-evidence source (DESIGN-RULINGS §1.2). `GEN_WORKER_FORBID_CPU_OFFLOAD` is unaffected — its real reader is the placement boundary in `models/memory.py`, which is now its only one. (2) `gen_worker.api.payload_constraints` (`Clamp`, `apply_payload_constraints`, `apply_schema_constraints`) is deleted. It claimed *"clamping is enforced server-side post-decode"* and had no serve-path call site at all; its one caller, the local CLI runner, discarded the result inside a bare `except`, and a `get_type_hints` failure silently clamped nothing. Zero uses fleet-wide. msgspec's own decode validation is, and always was, the whole payload contract. (3) `aot_mint.lifted_torch_gap` is deleted: the lifted-LoRA torch floor is an **image** fact settled once by the build gate into `endpoint.lock` (`aot_preconditions.static_mint_preconditions`), and the mint's second spelling of it could only ever agree with the wheel that gate already cleared — or disagree, which is the defect class. Its stated reason to exist, the mint-request CLI, died with the forge (§4.28).

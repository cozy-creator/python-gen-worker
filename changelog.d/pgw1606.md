### `ctx.load_pipeline` and the boot lane-selection ladder (pgw#1606)

A multi-lane `Model` class could not boot on a pod. `LoadedEndpoint.lane`
raised for any model declaring more than one lane, and the only writers of the
contract that would have silenced it are the local CLI and the daemon — the
production entrypoint builds `Worker(...)` with no `lane=` at all. That refusal
is why no endpoint in the fleet has ever declared two lanes.

Picking among declared lanes is platform work and now has a home:

* `serving/lane_ladder.py` ranks the declared lanes against the card, the
  host's own kernel gates and what the deploy staged, and answers a
  `ResolvedLane` naming the chosen lane, the reason, and the rejected rungs IN
  ORDER. It carries pgw#1599's declared lane verbatim, so `request=`,
  `resident=` and the derived `min_sm` keep exactly one producer.
* `ctx.load_pipeline(PipelineCls)` is the one spelling an endpoint writes. No
  dtype argument, no quantization if-tree, no hardware read at the author's
  site — the platform materializes modules per the resolved lane's contract
  and then PROVES it did: a quantized lane that converted nothing, a
  half-converted one, and a baseline lane carrying stray quantized leaves are
  each refused by name.
* The upcast-from-fp8-download rung is now CHOSEN rather than entered because
  a gate failed, and it reports a measured transfer saving instead of an
  assertion. It declines to claim a saving it cannot measure.
* No artifact for any lane resolves to a priced conversion ask, never a
  refusal.
* `DeployBinding` carries the staged tree, the bind gate's verdict and the
  wire size PER LANE CONTRACT. A single-lane deployment carries no map and is
  unchanged.

Acceptance (d) is measured rather than assumed: `serving/lane_fence.py` is the
one AST reader for "does this branch on hardware or numerics", split out
exactly as `load_marks_compile` was so a torch-free repo-side gate does not
write a second walker. It carries BOTH scopes on purpose — of the 23 branches
across the five endpoints that have them, 21 live one function OUTSIDE
`load()`, so a fence scoped to `load()` bodies passes four of those five today
with every branch intact.

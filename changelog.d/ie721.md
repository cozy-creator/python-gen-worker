- **ie#721: a content-shared component is never moved to the host.** A module
  reaching a pipeline through `provision.load_slot`'s `components=` is loaded
  once and aliased into every lane sharing its content address, so an offload
  rung that moved it to the host stranded every co-resident consumer on the
  device — a fatal `mat1 is on cuda:0, mat2 on cpu` in the middle of a generate,
  measured on krea-2, qwen-image, z-image and hidream-o1-image. The invariant
  was never new; it was enforced only as a side effect of `Resources.strict_vram`
  refusing every CPU-touching rung, and th#1867 deleted that declaration. It now
  has an enforcer of its own, taking a MEASURED fact (this object was injected as
  a shared component) rather than an author's claim: `shared_component_names`
  joins `_dtype_fragile_vae` in `unhookable_components`, the union that
  `_exclude_from_cpu_offload` and `_apply_group_offload` both honor. The two
  terms are independently sufficient and were red-proven separately by severing
  each arm alone. `place_pipeline` gained `resident_excluded` — **diagnostic
  only**, for operator visibility beside `oom_demotions`/`fit_needed_gb`; the
  published-input seam was retired once pgw#1265 landed a headroom check reading
  the driver's own free-memory figure, which already includes these bytes by
  observation. Wiring it into a budget check would rebuild the retired seam.

- **pgw#501: an endpoint that declares `lora_bucket > 0` now PROVES it can serve adapters, at build.** `peft` is not a gen-worker dependency and should not be — an endpoint that serves adapters declares that in its own image. What was missing is the platform proving the declared capability exists. It did not: `models/fp8_storage` imports `peft.tuners.{loha,lokr,lora}.layer` at call time and `utils/lora` routes overlays through diffusers' `load_lora_weights`, which hard-requires the package, so a missing `peft` surfaced as an `ImportError` at the FIRST OVERLAY — after acquisition, after the weights download, on a paid GPU pod. Discovery now stamps an `adapter_backend` precondition per declaring family, and `discovery.validation` turns a refusal into a build error naming the family, the declared bucket and the fix. **Live finding at the same time: `inference-endpoints/wan-2.2` declares `lora_bucket=128`/`64` and its lock contains no `peft` at all** — its adapter overlays cannot work today, and this check is what says so before a pod is bought.

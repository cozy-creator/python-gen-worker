- **pgw#1176: THE ATOM IS ONE GRAPH CLASS.** The compile loop compiled exactly one toy family in
  its entire life because it was built around a 36-entry all-or-nothing transaction — "the cell" —
  whose identity, adoption, durability, verification, arming and advertisement were all the same
  unit. Weight-free compile (pgw#1080) removed every reason for that unit to be big and the design
  never renegotiated. Three changes, landed together because splitting them creates a broken
  intermediate:

  **Identity.** `ek1 = digest(graph = class_hash × sm × toolchain)`, per entry. **The `envelope`
  axis LEAVES identity** and becomes a derived contract-manifest fact. `envelope_facts` digests the
  *union* of shapes/text_lens/guidance across the bundle — a property of the collection, not of any
  computation — so adding one aspect ratio re-keyed all 36 sdxl entries although 35 trace
  byte-identically (measured on `origin/master` @ `4dfdcd60`: two byte-identical classes moved
  `ck1-c4c134db…` → `ck1-48512ea3…` for one extra shape row). Per entry, the shape facts that
  genuinely affect tracing are already inputs to that class's graph hash — including the one real
  edge, a widened *dynamic* range, which re-keys through that class's own hash instead of punishing
  its siblings. `combined_graph_hash` demotes to `cell_key.manifest_digest`: a telemetry/coverage
  label the hub folds compile-health rows under, deliberately sm- and toolchain-free, and refused
  by `is_key` so nothing can resolve, download, verify or arm it.

  **Artifact.** Format 3: one `.pt2` + one `entry` block + its literals per graph class. The
  multi-entry `model.pt2` tarball, the `entries` map, and `combined_graph_hash` as identity are
  DELETED. `boot_key.fold` returns a key SET plus a manifest, so §4.27's <60 s target, the memo and
  boot-time adoption survive **strengthened** — a partial resolve now helps, where the cell key made
  30-of-36 a total miss and a full re-mint.

  **Arm.** The all-or-nothing arm is DELETED: `load_and_wrap`'s bind-all-then-wrap, the whole-cell
  key restatement, and `is_armed`'s every-target rule. `aot_serve.arm_entry` admits, binds and
  registers ONE class into a live `EntryDispatch` **registry** that entries join as they arm and
  leave when they de-arm; `disarm_entry` and the serve-path fail-soft de-arm cost one class, not the
  pipeline. The old invariant — *"a cell that cannot arm one of its graph classes arms none of
  them, because a partially served contract would be a silent subset of what the cell key
  advertises"* — is not weakened but made **vacuous**: a key advertises one class now, so nothing is
  left that CAN lie. This is what answers incremental compile-and-adopt.

  **And the constant clone is gone (pgw#1177, measured).** `target_constant_pool` handed back
  `value.detach().clone()` per FQN; `update_constant_buffer(user_managed=True)` makes no copy of its
  own, so that clone was the **only** copy in the system — one full duplicate of the target's
  weights held for the life of the arm, ≈5.1 GiB on sdxl's single `unet` target, in direct
  contradiction of §4.33 step 4. Its justification ("a post-arm resident mutation cannot silently
  change an armed cell") was backwards: eager sees the mutation, so it is the un-mutated compiled
  entry that diverges. What the clone also did — normalise contiguity — survives explicitly, per
  tensor, because an AOTI container takes a raw pointer.

  A deliberate, permanent SUBSET is now a legitimate steady state rather than a stage on the way to
  coverage. That is not only a latency story: pgw#1177 measured ~0.75 GiB of device memory per
  resident AOTI container, so a pod that arms its hot classes and leaves the cold ones eager holds a
  handful of containers instead of 36, on purpose. And "per entry" is the granularity of the
  ARTIFACT, never of the compile CHILD — pgw#1177 measured ~39 s of `env_seal.establish()` per fresh
  child, ~23 minutes across 36 entries, so a child that compiles several entries in sequence is
  strictly better and nothing here forbids it.

  `ck1 → ek1` is a §1.34 third-category re-key — a name persisted as an ADDRESS — and is executed as
  one coordinated cut with the hub schema, the resolve/publish wire, the
  `Arm.graph_contract_digest` proto change and the corpus purge. Priced now: the adoptable corpus is
  one toy family, so it costs one re-mint of a 3-class toy, and it can never be this cheap again.

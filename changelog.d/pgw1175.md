- **`mint_budget.py` is DELETED, whole, with every call site** (§4.33). It was a prediction layer
  standing where an attempt belongs: five peak banks, monotone ratchets, a sticky decline, and an
  arithmetic whose leading term charged the compiling child for `allocated` — the **parent's**
  resident weights, which the `free_bytes` it was compared against already excludes. The
  justification (*"a MEASUREMENT of the resident set the child provably re-holds"*) stopped being
  true at `fc77b923`, when every production mint became weight-free. It told wan-2.2 it needed
  **113.19 GiB**, z-image **77 GiB** and flux.2-klein-4b/-9b **49/85 GiB**, and all four were
  recorded *hardware-unsatisfiable*; §4.33 retracts those verdicts. A full sdxl mint costs ~8 GiB.
- **K = f(cores, one measured child RSS).** `entry_workers` loses its VRAM bound and everything
  built on it — `DeviceFacts`/`device_facts`, `DeviceProbeError`, `CardCensus`/`card_census`,
  `own_reserved_now`, `own_device_high_water`, `entry_budget_bytes`, `_apply_simultaneity_bound`,
  `_spawn_admitted`'s device ask, `note_residents_released`'s regrant, `_rewiden`, and the
  estimated-vs-measured device basis plumbing. The card is still asked ONE question — *does it
  exist* — because the GPU-benchmark lock is a correctness property of the emitted bytes. Measured
  on a 32-core / 64 GiB pod with a 2 GiB measured child: a 36-entry sdxl cell goes from K=1-2 to
  **K=8**.
- **The adopt is attempted, not predicted.** `mint_budget.adopt_headroom` (both call sites) and the
  sticky `note_adopt_declined` are gone. `aot_serve.load_and_wrap` now binds each entry inside a
  CUDA-OOM guard: a card that genuinely cannot hold the runners returns a typed
  `insufficient_adopt_vram` refusal **naming the entry that did not fit**, before the first live
  mutation, and the pod serves eager. The guard walks the cause chain, because
  `ArtifactRunner.bind` re-labels every `RuntimeError` out of `load_constants` as a CONTRACT
  verdict and `torch.OutOfMemoryError` is one — on the old tree a full card condemned a correct
  cell.
- **The `cell_adopt_budget` measurement is KEPT** (pgw#1168) and unhooked from the bank it fed. It
  is the only instrument that answers where a loaded cell's device memory goes, which is what
  §4.33's ~8 GiB target has to be checked against.
- Also deleted: `MintRequest.vram_cap_bytes` and `mint_child.cap_vram` (the child's
  `set_per_process_memory_fraction` ceiling was `co_residency().cap_bytes`, which pinned two real
  mints at 11.09 GiB on cards with 21.48 GiB free); `MintRequest.entry_device_peak_bytes`;
  `mint_delegate.DECLINED` and `executor._MintDeclined` (nothing refuses a mint in advance any
  more); the boot-time `mint_budget.probe` gate; `aot_mint._entry_device_bytes`. The surviving bank
  is the host-RSS one, in the new `mint_workers` module, and it is K's divisor.

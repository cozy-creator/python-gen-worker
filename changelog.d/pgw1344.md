- **The HF→GGUF tensor plane reads and writes through tensorfs, and the GGUF composes through the
  store.** The property is not that the conversion is cheaper — it is that a tensor whose GGUF bytes
  equal its safetensors bytes is admitted under the digest it ALREADY HAS, so a GGUF flavor of a
  resident model costs only the tensors that genuinely change. `tensorfs`'s consumption plane
  advances 333bb10 → 21cec66 to pick up `writer.py`: `TensorWriter` composes a snapshot one tensor
  at a time, in safetensors or GGUF, on the seal planner's own object grid. One upstream rewrite is
  real and declared — `TensorReader._map` maps through a compiled extension upstream, which pgw#1310
  rules out of a source-vendored wheel, so `_MappedObject` restores the mapping in pure Python via
  PEP 688's `__buffer__`, preserving both properties upstream needed (a weak-referenceable handle,
  and a buffer that outlives the reader exactly as long as a view of it does). `VENDORED.toml`
  records it and the digest fence covers it. `convert/gguf_native.py` is the conversion itself:
  tensors through `TensorReader`, names through gguf-py's own `TensorNameMap`, llama's rope permute
  on raw elements, 1-D and `*_norm.weight` pinned to F32 as `prepare_tensors` does, casts through
  torch so the rounding is the fleet's.

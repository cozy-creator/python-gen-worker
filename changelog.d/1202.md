- Typing: `mypy` now runs **strict by default** over `src/gen_worker`. A module
  added from here on is born strict; the 101 modules that were not clean at
  adoption carry a named, single-flag exemption in `pyproject.toml`. Measured at
  adoption: 413 `--strict` errors in 101 of 266 modules, so 165 modules were
  already strict-clean and are now locked there. `check_untyped_defs`,
  `disallow_incomplete_defs`, `strict_equality`, `extra_checks` and
  `disallow_subclassing_any` cost zero errors and are simply on — which means
  the `gen_worker.convert.*` untyped function bodies, previously unchecked
  entirely, are checked now.
- New CI guard `scripts/lint_mypy_ratchet.py` (`fast gates`): the per-module
  exemption lists may shrink but never grow, `strict = true` may not disappear,
  and a new kind of opt-out must be recorded before it can be used.

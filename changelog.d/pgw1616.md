### A serve lane declares its QUANTIZATION, and the dtype table prices every one (pgw#1616)

Vendors tensorfs `51adc50`. Two documents that could not be declared at all now
can: `sdxl.diffusers-nvfp4-flat@1` declares `float4_e2m1fn` and
`qwen3.6-27b-mtp.gguf-ud-q4-k-xl@1` declares `q4_k`. Under the always-required
`lanes=`, dtype-less meant *undeclarable*, so the GGUF endpoint was blocked by a
field nobody had to omit.

The reasoning that kept them dtype-less — "block-scaled/container formats have no
single torch spelling" — was true about tensor container types and wrong about the
field. `dtype` names the lane's quantization, which is why
`sdxl.diffusers-fp8-rowwise@1` declares `float8_e4m3fn` over 257 declarations of
which only 36 are fp8.

* `DTYPE_MIN_SM` learns the ggml block quants at floor `0`. Zero is the right
  answer, but it was arriving via the unknown-dtype default — and **the two zeros
  mean opposite things**: "measured: no floor" and "never heard of it" are the same
  return value, so an absent entry prices a lane by accident, silently, in the
  permissive direction.
* `FLOOR_LOSING_SPELLINGS` refuses `float4_e2m1fn_x2` by name. It is the packed-pair
  type torch actually ships (`float4_e2m1fn` does not exist in torch), so it reads as
  the correction someone will eventually make — and it resolves, drops the sm100
  floor, and puts an nvfp4 lane on Ampere. It is refused rather than aliased to 100,
  because aliasing works and lets the wrong spelling spread.
* Both exemptions in the dtype fence are gone, and neither was removed from memory:
  the self-deleting waiver went red naming its own entry, and the GGUF exemption's
  premise was falsified upstream. `test_the_nvfp4_lane_is_declarable_and_floors_at_blackwell`
  now runs against the real document instead of skipping.

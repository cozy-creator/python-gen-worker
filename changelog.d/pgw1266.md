- **pgw#1266 (ie#718): MiniMax-H3's two VAEs are fp32-pinned, so the producer
  half stops casting them.** `families.facts.COMPONENT_DTYPES` held exactly ONE
  entry — `AutoencoderKLWan` — since it was created, so
  `cast_exempt_components` would have narrowed `AutoencoderKLMiniMaxH3` and
  `AutoencoderKLMiniMaxH3Audio` on any `dtype: "bf16"` clone and
  `verify_produced_tree` would have published the result without a word. Both
  classes now carry an fp32 fact with the measurement behind it, which is what
  the table is for.

  The evidence is upstream's own and ours, not an inference. H3's video VAE
  class pins `_keep_in_fp32_modules` and states that the verified decode
  recipe is **fp16 autocast over fp32 weights** — the compute is already fp16,
  so narrowing the weights buys bandwidth at three fewer mantissa bits than
  the arithmetic uses; our banked ie#621 arm measured the fp16-WEIGHT case
  (strictly wider than bf16) at **74.97 dB PSNR min / 0.0186 max abs against
  the fp32 decode, at 0.94x** — worse on quality AND slower. The audio VAE is
  a DAC/BigVGAN stack upstream measures as **~20 dB quieter under bfloat16**,
  and it decodes at its own parameter dtype with no autocast to widen it back.

  `_keep_in_fp32_modules` is not a substitute for this table and never was: it
  refuses a DOWNCAST of fp32-on-disk bytes and can restore nothing from
  bf16-on-disk bytes. Without the pin the truncation is permanent, and
  invisible at every gate — which is the exact failure mode pgw#1133 built
  this machinery for, reproduced on a second model.

  This is why the −5.21 GB H3 VAE diet leg (ie#718) is REFUSED rather than
  measured: no GPU second was spent, because the free provenance check
  answered it. `tests/test_h3_vae_dtype_pins_pgw1266.py` drives the real
  `build_flavor_tree`, the real publish gate and the real table — 5 of 7 red
  on the parent commit.

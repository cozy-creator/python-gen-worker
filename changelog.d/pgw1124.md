- **pgw#1124: a boot-trace child holds no card, and a virtual structure is no longer counted as a failed CPU rollback.** Two independent defects made every boot-trace child of two live families fail 100% of the time (qwen-image 14/14, flux.2-klein-9b 9/9, `CUDA OOM left the pipeline mixed-device`), so their published cells were minted and never adopted. (1) The child ran the serving placement ladder, pushing the slot's REAL non-target components — qwen-image's 15.5 GiB text encoder, its VAE — onto the card the serving parent already occupies; it now loads `place=False` and proves the property with a typed `real_weights_resident` refusal carried by the pgw#1116 `boot_adopt` event vocabulary. (2) `device_mismatches` counted the deliberately-virtual compile target as "not on cpu", so the OOM rollback could never succeed and `place_pipeline` raised instead of demoting a rung — a recoverable ladder step was fatal, on every child, deterministically. A pgw#1080 structure-only component is exempt from the placement walk (it declines `_apply` by construction and its buffers belong to the traced graph); a meta tensor OUTSIDE one is still reported, because that is an unmaterialized load. Fix (2) also restores the OOM ladder for the MINT child, which composes structure-only and does place.

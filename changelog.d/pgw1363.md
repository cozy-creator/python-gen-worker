pgw#1363: the word "cell" is retired for compiled artifacts (Paul, 2026-08-18).
The vocabulary is graph class / compiled graph / cg-key, and the internal
surface now speaks it: `cell_adopt`/`cell_resolve`/`fleet_cells`/
`local_cell_store` are `compiled_graph_adopt`/`compiled_graph_resolve`/
`fleet_compiled_graphs`/`local_compiled_graph_store`, `CellPublisher` is
`CompiledGraphPublisher`, `registry.CompileCell` is `CompileContract`, and
tests/docs/prose follow. Wire spellings the hub owns are untouched and move
with the proto lane: proto fields (`cell_key`, `cell_ref`, `cell_lookups`, …),
the `jit_cell`/`aot_cell` serving modes, the `cell_*` boot phases,
`compile_cell_failed`, the `cell_read_*`/`cell_publish` claims,
`GEN_WORKER_LOCAL_CELLS_DIR` and the on-disk `aot-cells/` + `cell.tar.gz`
names. cg-key derivation inputs and the `ck1-` scheme are untouched.

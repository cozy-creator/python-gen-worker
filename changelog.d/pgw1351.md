- **pgw#1351: a pod now reports what it DOWNLOADED, so warm-boot dedup is a query instead of a guess.** e2e#1892's full journey set out to measure warm-boot dedup across three pods and had to report the absence of an instrument: `worker_activity_events` carried no per-pod wire-byte counter, and all three pods reported the same `tree on disk 12.93 GiB` — a figure identical on a pod that fetched everything and a pod that fetched nothing. The pull path knew the numbers the whole time and none of them left the pod. A snapshot pull now emits one completed `snapshot_pull` event (`gen_worker/snapshot_pull.py`) carrying `snapshot`, `requested_objects`, `tree_bytes`, `fetched_objects`, `fetched_bytes`, `resident_objects`/`resident_bytes`, `filled_objects`/`filled_bytes`, `late_resident_objects` and `accounted_objects`, served verbatim by the th#1839 route with no hub change. **Pod-local residency and an endpoint-volume fill are counted apart**, because folding them would make the pod's warm fraction a property of the volume; `fetched_bytes` comes from the transfer's own report rather than the grant sizes, so it is what moved and not what the manifest weighs.

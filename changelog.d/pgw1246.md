- **pgw#1246 ⇄ th#1941 (th#1934 Stage 2, PAIRED HARDCUT): the hub sends a
  RESOLVED VARIANT MANIFEST and the worker fetches exactly that.** The wire
  stops being keyed by ref and starts being keyed by the COMPOSED MANIFEST
  DIGEST. `ModelBinding.components` — `map<string, string> components = 5`, the
  only wire syntax for *"load X but not part of X"* — is DELETED with its
  number and name reserved; `ModelBinding.manifest_digest = 9` is the fetch
  pointer and `ref` survives as the display/audit label. `Snapshot.digest` is
  re-documented as that composed digest (fetch identity, dispatch key AND the
  worker's snapshot directory name), `Snapshot.provenance = 3` carries
  audit-only `ResolvedProvenance`/`ComponentOrigin`, and
  `DesiredResidency.snapshots` / `RunJob.snapshots` keep their numbers while
  their MAP KEY changes from canonical ref to composed manifest digest. Two
  resolutions of one repo that differ in their component sources are two
  entries with two digests and cannot interfere — the th#1933 class is
  unrepresentable, not merely fixed. No compatibility story, deliberately:
  pre-launch hardcut, both halves land together.

- **pgw#1246: `wire_snapshots.index_snapshots` is the one place the two

  to two different composed manifests in ONE message raises
  `AmbiguousManifestError` — a typed, deterministic refusal instead of the
  silent one-entry mutation th#1933 was.
